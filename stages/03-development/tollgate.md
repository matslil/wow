# Development Maturity Tollgate

Assess requirements and model integrity, traceability, architecture,
implementation, integration, verification, validation, defects, reproducibility,
operational preparation, and residual risk. Ask the designated human whether to
authorize Production and Transition.

Confirm that individual and set-level requirement quality has been assessed;
verification methods and cases provide appropriate coverage; later insights have
been propagated through affected upstream and downstream items; and larger
changes have a recorded holistic consistency review.
