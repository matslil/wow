# Concept Starter

Required outcomes are viable concepts, meaningful comparison including a
non-software response where credible, a selected concept and rationale, initial
boundary and outcomes, principal constraints and risks, and a recommendation.

Projects may tailor modeling depth, trade-study method, roles, and artifact
layout. They must preserve the decision evidence and alternatives considered.
