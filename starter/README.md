# Starter Layer

Starter content accelerates setup. Exploration and Concept methods, roles, and
artifacts may be substantially tailored because problem discovery varies by
project. Their required outcomes and core tollgate authority remain mandatory.

Development through Retirement use the prescribed stage processes. Projects may
add profiles, evidence, checks, or assurance, but may not weaken their workflow,
traceability, PR, or tollgate rules.

Record each starter disposition as `accepted`, `modified`, `replaced`,
`not-applicable`, or `deferred`, with rationale, risk, compensating evidence,
trigger, and approving authority where applicable.
